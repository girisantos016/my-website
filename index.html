01 / About

Teaching, research, and technology with practical purpose.

Santosh Giri is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electronics and Computer Engineering at Pulchowk Campus, Institute of Engineering, Tribhuvan University, Nepal. He coordinates the MSc Computer Engineering program specializing in Network and Cyber Security.

His work includes AI-based detection systems, cultural heritage applications, 3D reconstruction, curriculum development, faculty training, and technical consultancy. He has supervised 25+ bachelor's-level engineering projects and 15+ master's theses.
