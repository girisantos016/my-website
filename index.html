01 · About

Teaching, research, and technology applied to real-world problems.

Santosh Giri is an Assistant Professor of Computer Engineering in the Department of Electronics and Computer Engineering at Pulchowk Campus, Institute of Engineering, Tribhuvan University, Nepal.

His academic work spans artificial intelligence, machine learning, computer vision, deep learning, generative AI, and related computing disciplines. He combines undergraduate and postgraduate teaching with research supervision, curriculum development, funded research, faculty development, and technical consultancy.

He coordinates the MSc Computer Engineering program specializing in Network and Cyber Security. His research includes automatic monument detection, 3D reconstruction of cultural heritage, and AI-based detection and non-lethal deterrence systems.

03 · Selected Research Projects

Research from concept to implementation.

Selected projects are summarized here; project status and outputs are stated conservatively.

01

UGC Faculty Research Grant · FRG-81/82-Engg-05 · NPR 400,000

AI-Based Rhesus Macaque Detection and Repellent System

Principal Investigator for research into AI-assisted rhesus macaque detection combined with a non-lethal ultrasonic deterrence prototype.

ApproachYOLO-based object detection, image dataset development, and prototype testing
ToolsYOLOv8n, YOLOv11 comparison, Kaggle GPU, IC 555 ultrasonic prototype
StatusResearch and prototype development ongoing; related manuscript under review
02

Computer Vision · Cultural Heritage

Automatic Monument Detection

Transfer-learning-based recognition of selected monuments of Patan Durbar Square using a curated image dataset and a lightweight convolutional architecture.

ApproachTransfer learning, MobileNetV2, data augmentation, and class-wise analysis
FocusVisual recognition of selected cultural heritage monuments
OutputJournal article in the International Journal of Artificial Intelligence (IJAI), 2026
03

Research · 3D Computer Vision

Efficient 3D Scene Reconstruction

Research on reconstruction of 3D scenes from multi-view RGB images using an optimized Gaussian Splatting approach, including cultural heritage settings.

ThemeMulti-view reconstruction, Gaussian Splatting, and optimization
OutputIEEE Access, 2025
