01 · About

An academic practice grounded in teaching, research, and application.

Santosh Giri is an Assistant Professor of Computer Engineering at the Department of Electronics and Computer Engineering, Institute of Engineering, Tribhuvan University, Pulchowk Campus.

His academic work spans artificial intelligence, machine learning, computer vision, cybersecurity and engineering-oriented technology. Alongside teaching, he contributes to research projects, postgraduate coordination, academic workshops, and professional activities that connect computing concepts with practical problems.

He currently coordinates the MSc Computer Engineering (Network and Cyber Security) program and teaches across undergraduate and postgraduate computing courses.

03 · Research Projects

Selected work

A small selection of projects and research directions. Add further projects as they are completed.

01

UGC Faculty Research Grant · FRG-81/82-Engg-05

AI-Based Rhesus Macaque Repellent

Research on an AI-assisted system for rhesus macaque detection combined with a non-lethal ultrasonic deterrence prototype.

ApproachYOLO-based object detection; image dataset development; ultrasonic prototype
ToolsYOLOv8n · YOLOv11 comparison · Kaggle GPU · IC 555 · Oscilloscope
OutcomeDetection model development and prototype frequency validation; manuscript under review
02

Computer Vision · Cultural Heritage

Automatic Monument Detection

Transfer-learning based recognition of selected monuments of Patan Durbar Square using a curated image dataset and a lightweight convolutional architecture.

ApproachTransfer learning · MobileNetV2 · data augmentation · class-wise analysis
FocusRobust visual recognition of selected heritage monuments
StatusAccepted in IAES International Journal of Artificial Intelligence
03

Research · 3D Computer Vision

Efficient 3D Scene Reconstruction

Research on efficient reconstruction of 3D scenes from multi-view RGB imagery using optimized Gaussian-based representation.

ThemeMulti-view reconstruction · Gaussian representation · optimization
VenueIEEE Access
